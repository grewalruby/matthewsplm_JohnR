PLM Business Process workflow
Workflow status and approval tracking

Integration Capabilities

Directly associate Quality Non-Conformance Reports with PLM engineering items, enabling full traceability from quality events to engineering changes and production updates.
Quality events automatically trigger PLM business processes such as Engineering Change Requests (ECR), Engineering Change Notices (ECN), or New Product Introductions (NPI) with configurable rules.
Configurable multi-level approval workflows ensure proper review gates for engineering changes initiated from quality events. Full audit trail maintained.
